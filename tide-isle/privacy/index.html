Privacy Policy

Tide Isle · Izasa, LLC · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Tide Isle is a child-directed play app published by Izasa, LLC (“we,” “us”). This policy explains how the Tide Isle app handles information. It applies only to Tide Isle. Focus Shift, also published by Izasa, LLC, has its own policy at focusshift.app/privacy.

The short version

A child can use Tide Isle without an account. We do not collect personal information from children, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools. If you allow the microphone, audio is processed on the device. We do not record it or upload it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask children or caregivers to create a Tide Isle account. We do not ask for a child’s name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, or location.

  • No third-party advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal information
  • No in-app purchases

Play does not depend on providing personal information.

Information that stays on the device

Tide Isle may save settings and simple play summaries on the device — for example, caregiver preferences and counts of how activities are used — so a parent can review them in a parent area. That information stays on the device. We do not receive a copy of it.

A parent can delete the local summary from the parent area. Deleting the app removes the information Tide Isle stored on that device.

Microphone

Microphone access is optional. If you allow it, Tide Isle uses the microphone on the device to notice sound for optional voice play. Audio is processed on the device only. We do not record it, store a copy of it, or send it to us or to a speech or cloud service.

The app works if you decline microphone access. You can turn the microphone off later in the device’s Settings.

Children’s privacy

Tide Isle is directed to children. We follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email support@focusshift.app and we will delete it.

If you email us

If a parent or caregiver emails us, we use that email address and the message only to respond. Please avoid including a child’s full name or other personal details unless you need them to explain the request. We keep the message only as long as needed to handle it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Parents’ choices

  • Decline microphone access, or turn it off later in Settings
  • Use the parent area, behind a parental gate, to change settings or delete local play summaries
  • Delete the app to remove Tide Isle’s on-device information
  • Email us with a privacy question or deletion request

Apple

Tide Isle is offered through Apple’s App Store. Apple may process information under Apple’s privacy policy when you use an Apple device or the App Store. That processing is controlled by Apple. We do not receive a child’s personal information from Apple through Tide Isle.

How long information is kept

We do not keep a server copy of a child’s play or audio, because Tide Isle does not send that information to us. On-device information remains until a parent deletes it or deletes the app. Support emails are kept as described above.

Security

We do not operate a Tide Isle account system or a database of children’s personal information. Information stored on the device is protected by the device’s own security, including its passcode and Apple’s platform protections.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will post the updated policy here before it applies.
